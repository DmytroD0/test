Know what to watch for

Common health concerns after 40

Many age-related conditions develop quietly. Early awareness and regular screenings make a real difference.

🩸

Type 2 Diabetes

Keeping blood sugar in a healthy range helps prevent long-term complications.

🫁

Lung Health

Stay active, avoid smoke and keep up with recommended vaccines.

🦴

Osteoporosis

Calcium, vitamin D and strength training help keep bones dense.

👓

Vision Changes

Regular eye exams catch cataracts and glaucoma early.

🪥

Oral Health

Healthy gums are linked to overall health — floss daily and see your dentist.

🌙

Sleep Quality

Consistent, restful sleep supports mood, metabolism and memory.

Move with ease

Caring for your joints & bones

Small daily adjustments can keep you moving comfortably for years to come.

01
🦵

Knees

Maintain a healthy weight, strengthen the muscles around your knees and choose low-impact activities like cycling or swimming.

Less strainBetter mobility
02
🦶

Ankles & Feet

Wear supportive shoes and stretch your calves and arches daily to stay steady on your feet.

Fewer achesBetter balance
03
✋

Hands & Wrists

Gentle hand exercises and regular breaks from repetitive tasks help keep fingers flexible.

Stronger gripLess stiffness
04
🧘

Back & Hips

Good posture when sitting, standing and lifting — plus core work — protects your spine.

Posture supportEasier walking
Know your numbers

Keep your blood pressure in check

High blood pressure often has no symptoms. Knowing where you stand lowers your risk of heart disease and stroke.

Blood pressure categories

Adults, mm Hg (systolic / diastolic)
Normal<120 and <80
Elevated120–129 and <80
Stage 1 high130–139 or 80–89
Stage 2 high140+ or 90+
🥗

Try the DASH eating plan

Cut back on sodium and load up on fruits, vegetables, whole grains and low-fat dairy.

🚶

Stay active

Aim for 30 minutes of brisk walking or similar activity, five days a week.

🧘‍♀️

Manage stress

Deep breathing, time outdoors and good rest all help your body recover.

📈

Track at home

Log your readings and bring them to appointments so your doctor sees the full picture.

Heart health

Six habits your heart will thank you for

Heart disease is the leading cause of death in the U.S. — and many risk factors are within your control.

🐟

Eat heart-smart

Fatty fish, beans, nuts, olive oil and whole grains.

Supports healthy cholesterol
🚭

Quit smoking

Your risk starts dropping soon after your last cigarette.

Lower cardiovascular risk
🥂

Drink less

If you drink, keep it moderate — or skip it altogether.

Helps blood pressure
⚖️

Healthy weight

A BMI of 18.5–24.9 and a healthy waist size are good targets.

Less work for your heart
💊

Follow your plan

Take medications as prescribed and never stop without talking to your doctor.

Better long-term control
🩺

Annual checkup

Check cholesterol, blood sugar and blood pressure every year.

Catch issues early
Stay sharp

Keep your mind active at every age

Your brain stays adaptable throughout life. Give it something new to work on.

Read every day

Books, long articles, anything that makes you think and grows your vocabulary.

Play brain games

Chess, crosswords, sudoku and card games challenge memory and strategy.

Learn something new

A language, an instrument, a craft — novelty builds new connections.

Keep moving

Exercise boosts blood flow to the brain and supports mood.

💧

Stay hydrated

🛌

Sleep well

7–9 hours on a consistent schedule.

🥕

Eat the rainbow

🤝

Stay connected
